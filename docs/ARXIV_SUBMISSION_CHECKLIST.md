- `CHRISTUS_ORGANISM_ARXIV_PREPRINT.pdf` — 15-page PDF, no line numbers, single-column preprint layout.
- `CHRISTUS_ORGANISM_ARXIV_PREPRINT.docx` — editable source.

The PDF is generated from the DOCX with LibreOffice; it is suitable for arXiv
PDF-only submission. arXiv accepts PDF uploads, although a TeX source is
preferred by some authors. The editable DOCX/Markdown source is included for
revision.

## Before pressing submit

1. Confirm the spelling/order of the author name and the email address.
2. Confirm the affiliation.
3. Confirm the CC BY 4.0 license choice (or select another arXiv license).
4. Check that no arXiv identifier or DOI is invented in the PDF.
5. After arXiv assigns an ID, add it to the Nature cover letter and Nature
   manuscript title page.

## Honest scope note

The manuscript argues for an *incipient functional* organization. It does not
claim phenomenal consciousness. The title uses “functional consciousness”;
reviewers may contest the framing even though the text qualifies it.
